
# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** August 17, 2026

## 1. Commitment to Privacy
Extract XY is designed to prioritize user privacy in full compliance with Apple's App Store Review Guidelines regarding data minimization and user data protection.

## 2. Local Processing & No Server Collection
* **Zero Server Storage:** All Vision framework scanning, image processing, and data calculations occur strictly on your iOS device. 
* **No External Data Transfers:** Your images, plots, and digitized coordinate points are never sent to external servers or remote databases.

## 3. No User Accounts
Extract XY **does not require user registration or account creation**. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or personal identifying information.

## 4. Third-Party Services
To manage in-app purchase validation, Extract XY integrates with **RevenueCat**.
* **Data Handled:** RevenueCat processes anonymous app user IDs and receipt information strictly to verify and restore purchase entitlements.
* **No Tracking:** RevenueCat does not collect personally identifying information or track users across third-party apps and websites for advertising.
* For further detail, refer to [RevenueCat's Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/).

## 5. Clipboard Usage
When you export or copy digitized plot data, it is written locally to your device's clipboard so you can paste it into other iOS applications. Extract XY does not transmit or read clipboard contents outside of explicit user export actions.

## 6. Children's Privacy
The App does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## 7. Updates to Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any modifications will be effective upon posting the revised policy within the App or on our App Store product page.
