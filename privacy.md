
## Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** July 28, 2026

Your privacy is fundamental to us. **Extract XY is designed with a privacy-first architecture.**

### 1. No Account Creation
Extract XY does **not** require user accounts, logins, email registration, or passwords. You can use the App completely anonymously.

### 2. On-Device Data Processing
* **Image & Plot Data:** Photos, images, or documents you load into Extract XY for plot digitization are processed **strictly on your local device** using Apple’s Vision framework. 
* **No Cloud Uploads:** Your images, digitized coordinates, and calculated results are **never** uploaded to, stored on, or transmitted across any external servers or third-party cloud services.
* **Clipboard Usage:** When you copy digitized data to your device's clipboard, that data remains locally within your iOS ecosystem for you to paste into other applications. We do not track or monitor your clipboard contents.

### 3. Third-Party Services
Extract XY uses **RevenueCat** as a third-party service provider to manage In-App Purchase state, receipt validation, and purchase restoration. 

* **What RevenueCat Processes:** RevenueCat processes anonymous, transactional data (such as anonymous App User IDs, purchase history, and receipt tokens provided by Apple) solely to verify your purchase status and restore your access.
* **What RevenueCat Does NOT Collect:** RevenueCat does not receive, access, or store your plot images, digitized data, calculations, or personal identity details.
* You can review RevenueCat’s privacy practices at [https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

### 4. Analytics and Tracking
Extract XY does **not** use tracking cookies, cross-app tracking frameworks (IDFA), or third-party advertising frameworks. 

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the App or on our official store page.
